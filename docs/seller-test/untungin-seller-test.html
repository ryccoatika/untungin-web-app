Usability test · Untungin

Can a first-time seller get from zero to "my real profit"?

A one-on-one session with a real seller, on their own products. You watch them use Untungin without helping, note where they get stuck, and ask what they would want next. Five sessions are enough to find most of the big problems.

3–5 sellersOne at a time
35–45 minutesPer session
Their own productsNot the sample shop
You don't helpUnless they're stuck 2 min

What we want to learn

Three questions this test answers

  1. Setup: can a seller enter their materials and one real recipe without help, and do sdm/sdt/gelas make that easier?
  2. Understanding: do they understand what the dashboard tells them (HPP, untung bersih, titik impas, balik modal), and do they trust the numbers?
  3. What's next: which matters most to them: recording real daily sales, a summary for a bank loan (KUR), or something we haven't thought of?

Recruiting

Who to invite

  • People who sell food or drinks: a warung, kopi keliling, kue rumahan, katering kecil, jualan online.
  • At least two who have never calculated HPP; the app is built for them.
  • A mix of ages and of how comfortable they are with phones.
  • Avoid close friends and family: they will be polite instead of honest. A neighbour's neighbour is better.
  • Offer a small thank-you, such as Rp50.000 in e-wallet credit or a drink, and say so when you invite them.

Ask them to bring the details of one product they sell: its ingredients, roughly how much of each, what they pay for them, and the selling price. A receipt or a photo of their notes is perfect.

Before each session

Setup checklist

  • Device: a phone with Untungin open and already signed in to a fresh test account, so sign-in isn't part of the test. Until the app is deployed, use your laptop's browser at phone width, or your own phone on the same Wi-Fi.
  • Fresh account: a new Google test account per session, starting at the store setup screen, so nobody sees the previous person's data.
  • Notes: this page's note sheet (below), or paper. Write down what they say in their own words.
  • Recording: only if they agree. Screen recording plus audio is the most useful; never record their face without asking.
  • A second person to take notes helps, so you can watch closely.

Opening · 3 min

Introduction

"Terima kasih sudah mau datang. Saya sedang membuat aplikasi untuk membantu penjual menghitung modal, harga jual, dan untung. Hari ini yang diuji aplikasinya, bukan Bapak/Ibu, jadi tidak ada jawaban yang salah."

"Kalau ada yang membingungkan, itu justru yang paling saya cari. Tolong ceritakan apa yang dipikirkan sambil mencoba, misalnya 'saya cari tombol untuk…' atau 'ini maksudnya apa ya'."

"Saya akan sebisa mungkin tidak membantu, supaya saya tahu bagian mana yang perlu diperbaiki. Boleh saya rekam layarnya? Rekamannya hanya untuk saya."

Warm-up · 5 min

About their business

Ask before they touch the app. Their answers tell you what "success" means for them.

"Jualan apa saja? Sudah berapa lama?"

"Bagaimana cara menentukan harga jual sekarang?"

"Kira-kira untungnya berapa sebulan? Bagaimana tahunya?"

"Pernah mencatat modal atau pengeluaran? Pakai apa: buku, kalkulator, Excel, aplikasi?"

"Kalau menakar bahan, biasanya pakai apa: timbangan, sendok, gelas, atau kira-kira?"

Tasks · 25 min

The tasks

Read each task aloud, then stay quiet. Only use the hint ladder when they have been stuck for about two minutes or ask directly.

Step 1 · Wait"Apa yang sedang dicari?"
Step 2 · Nudge"Menurut Bapak/Ibu, kira-kira ada di bagian mana?"
Step 3 · ShowPoint to it, note "needed help", and move on.

T1Set up the store

≈ 3 min

"Bayangkan Bapak/Ibu baru mulai pakai aplikasi ini. Silakan buat toko untuk usaha Bapak/Ibu."

Watch for

  • Do they understand "target margin"? Do they read the explanation?
  • Hesitation over "hari buka per bulan".
  • Do they notice "Coba dengan data contoh"? Don't point it out.

Done when

They land on the dashboard of their own store.

T2Enter two real ingredients

≈ 5 min

"Masukkan dua bahan yang dipakai untuk produk yang Bapak/Ibu bawa, dengan harga belinya."

Watch for

  • Which unit they pick (kg, gram, liter, pcs) and whether it matches how they buy.
  • How they type prices: "15000", "15.000", "15rb"?
  • Do they understand "susut / terbuang (%)"?
  • For gula or tepung: do they see and fill "Berat 1 sdm (gram)"? Do they know the number?

Done when

Both materials are saved with a sensible cost per gram, ml or pcs.

T3Make their product and its recipe

≈ 7 min

"Sekarang buat produknya, lalu isi resepnya: bahan apa saja dan berapa banyak untuk satu kali bikin atau satu porsi."

Watch for

  • Do they understand "hasil per resep" (yield) and "per resep vs per porsi"? This is the biggest risk.
  • Do they reach for sdm, sdt or gelas on their own? If sugar has no spoon weight, do they follow the link to Bahan?
  • Do they add packaging (cup, plastik, sedotan) as an ingredient without being told?
  • Do they say the HPP looks right? Ask: "Masuk akal angkanya?"

Done when

The product shows an HPP the seller says is roughly right.

T4Price and sales estimate

≈ 3 min

"Isi harga jualnya sekarang dan kira-kira berapa yang terjual dalam sebulan."

Watch for

  • Do they notice the recommended price? Do they trust it or ignore it?
  • Can they estimate monthly sales, or do they think in days? Note what they say.
  • Do they use "Pakai harga rekomendasi"? Ask why or why not.

Done when

The product has a price and a monthly estimate.

T5Read the dashboard

≈ 4 min

"Menurut aplikasi ini, berapa untung bersih Bapak/Ibu sebulan? Dan berapa yang harus terjual per hari supaya tidak rugi?"

Watch for

  • Can they find and say both numbers in their own words?
  • Do they understand why profit looks lower than they thought? Costs, depreciation, their own salary.
  • Do they read the warnings ("Perlu diperhatikan")? Do they understand them?
  • Reaction to the 3D chart: helpful, confusing or decoration?

Done when

They state both numbers and say whether they believe them.

T6Try a "what if"

≈ 3 min

"Misalnya harga bahan naik 20%. Coba lihat, untungnya jadi berapa?"

Watch for

  • Do they find Simulasi? On a phone it's under Lainnya.
  • Do they understand "Sekarang" vs "Simulasi" and the difference?
  • Do they worry the change is saved? Watch for hesitation.

Done when

They read the simulated profit and the change.

T7Ask the AI Optional

≈ 3 min

"Ada fitur yang bisa memberi saran untuk usaha Bapak/Ibu. Coba minta sarannya, lalu ceritakan pendapatnya."

Watch for

  • Do they read the privacy note before pressing the button?
  • Is the advice useful, obvious, or wrong for their situation?
  • Do they try "Coba di simulasi"?

Done when

They read one piece of advice and say what they think of it.

Only if a Gemini key is set up on the device you're using. Each press uses one of the account's 3 daily analyses.

Wrap-up · 7 min

Closing questions

"Dari angka 1 sampai 5, seberapa mudah aplikasi ini dipakai? Kenapa?"

"Bagian mana yang paling membingungkan?"

"Ada angka yang Bapak/Ibu tidak percaya atau tidak mengerti?"

"Kalau aplikasi ini ada di HP, kapan Bapak/Ibu akan membukanya? Setiap hari, sebulan sekali, atau waktu mau ubah harga?"

Then lay these three cards in front of them, on paper or on screen, and ask them to put them in order of usefulness:

Catat penjualan harianRecord what actually sold each day and see real profit next to the estimate.
Ringkasan untuk pinjaman bankA one-page PDF of costs, prices, profit and payback period for a KUR application.
Catat belanja bahanRecord each purchase so material prices update automatically.

"Kenapa yang ini paling atas? Ada yang belum ada di sini tapi Bapak/Ibu butuhkan?"

"Kalau aplikasi ini berbayar, berapa yang wajar menurut Bapak/Ibu per bulan?"

After all sessions

Turning notes into decisions

  1. List every problem you saw. Count how many sellers hit each one.
  2. Rate each: Blocker they couldn't continue without help; Friction slowed or confused them; Polish noticed but didn't matter.
  3. Fix every blocker seen by two or more sellers before adding features.
  4. Use the card ranking and the "missing feature" answers to pick the next phase.
If you see…It suggests…
T3 fails for most sellers (yield, per resep vs per porsi)Simplify recipe entry before anything else, for example a guided "untuk berapa porsi?" step.
They distrust the profit number or think in daily salesRecording real daily sales is the next phase.
Several mention a bank, KUR or a loanBuild the one-page loan summary next.
They skip packaging or their own salaryMake the setup checklist more prominent, or ask about them during setup.
Nobody uses the 3D chart, or it confuses themDefault the chart to 2D.

Bring the notes back here and Claude can turn them into a ranked fix list and the next plan.
